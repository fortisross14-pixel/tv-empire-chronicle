# Audit pass 1: game logic, performance, functionality (2026-11-11)

Skills used: `.claude/skills/game-audit` (engine, economy, perf, functionality),
`save-systems` and `card-game` (awesome-gamedev-agent-skills). Three reviews ran in
parallel: engine rules and state integrity, economy and balance (10-seed, 7-year bot
simulations), and UI functionality and performance (Playwright on a 360px phone).

**Verdict:** no blockers through the UI. One latent softlock, 7 major and about 15 minor
issues were found; all majors and most minors are fixed in this pass. Performance is
healthy. The economy has large late-game problems that belong to the balancing wave.

## Performance (measured)

- `airWeek` 0.1–0.5 ms, flat over 4 simulated years; structuredClone ≤ 0.5 ms.
- Save size 37 KB after 1 year, 65 KB after 4 years (the uncapped part is the show list).
- Air Week click to paint 38–50 ms; tab switch 2–32 ms; typing 7 ms per key in the wizard.
- Bundle: 342 KB JS (108 KB gzip), 22 KB CSS. Only react and react-dom.
- The wizard's plan preview is now memoised on the plan's contents.

## Left for later waves

**Balancing wave** (from the economy review; numbers are 10-seed medians):
- No money ceiling after State: $6M by year 3, $85M by year 7; nothing to spend on.
  Overhead, affiliate prices and Advanced/AAA production costs need to scale with tier.
- Reputation gain sums over every slot, so it snowballs at State/National (100 by year 3).
  Normalise per slot or raise the National requirement.
- Starved starts spiral: house-only teams make shows whose critics cost reputation, and
  rivals gain +3 quality a year with no cap.
- Renewals can't keep novelty up (+25 vs 30–45 lost per season).
- National rights (Gridiron, Galaxy Wars) lose money at their prices.
- Glimmy indie field is unwinnable below National; the #1-network goal was never reached
  in simulation (share stuck near 9% against 3 rivals).
- Late-game dead zone: after year 3 only packs remain to buy.

**UI pass:** visual style (3 options prepared), toast above sheets, archive old shows.

**Design note:** the design said on-screen talent is locked to a show while it airs; the
engine (on purpose since wave 1) only locks people during production stages. DESIGN.md
is updated to match.
