# Visual and navigation polish (Wave 15)

Screens were reviewed on a phone (390 px) and a laptop (1280 px) with real mid-game
stations (Local week 30, State week 14, National week 10, played by the bot).

## Fixed

- **Crash:** the Guide tab crashed for State and National stations that owned a movie
  (sports-slot lookup on a movie). The error screen caught it; a safe helper now finds
  sports slots, with a regression test.
- **Main menu:** three clear choices (Load game, Start a mission, Start a sandbox);
  new games pick a save slot; replacing a used slot asks first.
- **Home:** actionable things first (mission, billboard, Up next, on air, in production,
  last week, station, goals), reference sections later (tonight's guide, cast, trophies,
  news). The Chronicle shows the latest 5 with "Show all news".
- **Billboard:** uses the poster without its frame (a TV-set frame used to cut the title
  and buttons), and no longer repeats "Air week" (the floating button does that); it
  offers Details and Guide instead.
- **Tonight's guide** names the movie on air instead of "Licensed film".
- **Guide tab:** Your shows first, then Sports rights, then a compact Movies list
  (grade, airings left, slot or "Not on air").
- **Studio:** the in-production card no longer overflows ("Post" label, wrapping footer).
- **Market:** no duplicate cash line.
- **Sheets:** Escape closes the top sheet only (event and result sheets that need an
  answer can't be dismissed).
- **Event choices** you can't afford are disabled with a reason (Wave 14).

## Feel and devices (second pass)

- **Touch vs mouse:** hover lifts only on devices with a fine pointer and hover; touch
  screens get press feedback (scale) on cards, posters, tiles and tabs, no sticky hover,
  no tap flash or double-tap delay; 40–44 px targets on coarse pointers.
- **Layouts:** phone portrait one column; phone landscape slim inline tab bar and HUD,
  taller sheets; tablet portrait one column with roomier sheets; tablet landscape and
  laptops a two-column Home (act on the left, station at a glance on the right, sticky);
  1240 px+ widens the page.
- **Motion:** tabs slide in; cash counts up/down with a floating "+$28K"; the week's net
  counts up in the ratings; WIN badges pop; the featured show drifts slowly; the Air week
  button eases in; confetti on a mission win. All off with "reduce motion".
