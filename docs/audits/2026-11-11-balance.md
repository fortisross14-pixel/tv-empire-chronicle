# Balance pass (Wave 16)

**Target:** a casual game. A challenge, never impossible: no early death spirals, each
tier a real goal, money that matters, the #1 crown hard but reachable.

Measured with `npm run playtest` (the bot plays like a careful player: best affordable
show, renewals, movies for empty slots, saves for licenses). Economy runs: Sandbox,
6 seeds, 6 years each.

## Before

| | |
|---|---|
| State | week ~129, then National **5 weeks later** (State skipped) |
| Reputation | 90 by year 2, 100 by year 3 (it summed over every slot) |
| Money | $35M profit a year at National, $88M banked by year 6 |
| National #1 | never (4th of 4 on whole-day share) |
| Awards | Indie field nearly unwinnable for local shows |

## After

| Difficulty | State | National | #1 crown | Loans | Bankrupt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Relaxed ($220K, softer rivals) | week ~46 | week ~123 | 2 of 6 (~week 207) | 0 | 0 |

| Tough ($90K, rivals ×1.08) | week ~97 | week ~195 | 3 of 6 (~week 214) | 0 | 0 |

Normal, yearly medians: Y1 Local $0.7M profit · Y2 State $6.7M · Y3 $8.4M ·
Y4 National $16M · Y6 $24M. Cash banked stays $1.5–3.5M while expanding (affiliates,
productions, licenses), then grows in the late game as a reward.

## What changed

- **Reputation** per slot is scaled to a 6-slot schedule, so 15–20 slots no longer earn
  3× faster. State ~55 after a year, National 60 needs real State play.
- **National license** $4M cash and reputation 60, fee $2.5M (was $1.5M / $1M): State now
  lasts about 1.5 years.
- **Money scales with the tier.** Running costs: Local $7K, State $38K, National $160K ×
  reach (a new network at 50% reach pays half; affiliates add reach and cost).
  Production costs ×1.8 at State, ×2.5 at National. National audience 10M households.
- **Affiliates** $500K, then +$250K each (10 affiliates ≈ $16M: a real late-game goal).
- **National prices:** national IP licenses ×3 (Galaxy Wars $2.7M), National movie pack
  $250K.
- **Welcome gift:** each license brings a free movie pack of the new tier, so the bigger
  schedule doesn't start empty.
- **Novelty:** a new season brings back 40 novelty (was 25); long runs fade slower (season
  cap −8 per season, floor 45).
- **Rivals:** yearly quality growth +2/year, capped at +8 (was +3/year, no cap); their
  shows aren't always brand new (novelty 35–90); National rivals about 6 quality lower.
- **Awards:** fields fit each level (Indie 40–63, State 54–76, Glimmy below National
  64–82, National Glimmys 72–90); yearly growth capped at +6.
- **The #1 crown** is won in **prime time**, 26 weeks in a row at #1 (Home shows the
  streak), the same yardstick as the missions.
- **Sandbox Tough** softened ($90K, rivals ×1.08): it was stuck at Local in half the runs.
- **New hint:** "Every show idea is taken" when all your format + topic pairs are in use
  (a player could get stuck re-running two shows).

## Missions after balance

All 24 re-run (6 seeds each). Most win 5–6/6; the hardest: House Rules 4/6, Critics'
Darling 6/6 (now spans two Indie nights), Coast to Coast 5/6 (4 affiliates).
Changed for the new numbers: Bleeding Money (lighter debt), Coast to Coast (4 affiliates,
$2.5M), Critics' Darling (starts in autumn, 80 weeks, two ceremonies), Back for More
(44 weeks), House Rules (90 weeks), Rights Rush ($800K). Preset lineups now use varied
format/topic pairs (repeated pairs wore each other out).

## Bot fixes found along the way (not game changes)

It bought only people packs (never new formats), always chose the most expensive
production level, never bought movies for empty slots, spent while saving for a license
or affiliate, and bid too low when it wanted rights.
