# Balance pass after the lot, step-by-step production and the new Market (2026-11-14)

Target: a casual game. A challenge, never a dead end. Measured with the playtest bot
(`npm run playtest`), which now plays more like a person when broke (renews an old show if it
has nothing else, buys a pack before building a cash cushion).

## What was wrong

- One Sandbox station in six sank to reputation 0 at Local and went bankrupt in year 5.
- Local and State sports paid back 2 to 7 times their price; National sports lost money.
- With the new sources of quality (a crew card per slider, optional parts) and IP ad income,
  a competent player reached State in 39 weeks and took the national crown in 11 of 12 runs.
- Play Ball and Sports Town were won in week one by buying rights. House Rules was won in 2
  of 6 runs. Several third stars were automatic; several Beginner ones were out of reach.

## Changes

| Area | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| State license | reputation 30, $250K, fee $200K | reputation 40, $350K, fee $250K |
| Rival show quality | Local 26–52, State 40–66, National 46–75; +2 a year up to +8 | Local 30–56, State 45–71, National 50–79; up to +10 |
| Sports prices | e.g. Ice Hawks $30K, State Hoops $100K, Gridiron $5M, Tennis $1.8M | a season earns roughly 2–3× its price: $60K, $260K, $2.4M, $600K |
| Optional parts | up to about +8 screen power each | up to about +6 |
| Marketing | $5K / $14K × production multiplier | $8K / $25K at Local, ×5 at State, ×25 at National |
| Out of money | bank loan, then game over | bank loan, then investors once (reputation −8), then game over |
| Stuck at the bottom | nothing | Arts Council grant: $60K and two free packs for a Local station with under $35K and reputation under 8 that has made 2+ shows; once a year |

Missions: Play Ball needs 4 games; Sports Town is now "broadcast 40 local games in a year";
House Rules has 104 weeks and $500K; National Ambition has 116 weeks and $450K. Star
bonuses retuned (Beginner ones loosened, automatic ones tightened).

## Results

Sandbox, 12 stations, 6 years:

| | Relaxed | Normal | Tough |
|---|---|---|---|
| State (median week) | 45 | 51 | 58 |
| National (median week) | 130 | 125 | 148 |
| #1 crown | 1 of 6 in 5 years | 4 of 12 in 6 years | 1 of 6 in 5 years |
| Bankrupt | 0 | 0 | 0 |

(Relaxed and Tough: 6 stations, 5 years.)

Missions, 6 stations each: every mission is won 6 of 6, except Empire (5 of 6, by design the
hardest). Two stars are the norm; the third is earned in roughly 1 to 4 runs of 6.

## Not measured

- A player who makes shows step by step and waits between steps (the bot plans a show at once).
- Marketing and release choices (the bot never buys marketing).
- National income is still high late (about $34K a year by year 6) with little to spend it on:
  a job for the next wave (more buildings, staff costs on the way to AAA).
